01Why sound

Cameras see what is in frame. Microphones hear around corners, through walls and in the dark. SonicSentinel turns that sound into events a person can act on.

02Pipeline

From microphone
to decision.

01/ 06
01

Capture

Upload a recording, or open the live monitor and stream a microphone in rolling two-second windows.

  • WAV · MP3 · FLAC · OGG · M4A
  • Up to 25 MB and 10 minutes
02

Clean

Mixed to mono, DC offset removed, resampled to 22.05 kHz, spectral-gate noise reduction, silence trimmed and the peak normalised.

  • Same steps for training and prediction
03

Segment

Cut into two-second segments that overlap by one second, so a short event is never split in half. Quiet segments are skipped.

  • 2 s windows · 1 s hop
04

Listen twice

A Python ensemble reads 193 acoustic features on the server. A Teachable Machine network reads a 43 × 232 spectrogram in your browser.

  • Neither model sees the other’s answer
05

Compare

The two probability sets are fused and compared. The result is a match, a weak match, a disagreement or an uncertain result.

  • =  ≈  ≠  ?
06

Act

Per-class alert rules decide severity, confirmation and escalation. Anything uncertain goes to a person in the review queue.

  • Every step is kept in the audit trail

08Questions

Asked often.

Which sounds can it recognise?

{% for c in classes %}{{ c.name }}{{ ", " if not loop.last else "." }}{% endfor %} Anything that fits none of them is reported as an unknown sound.

Which files can I analyse?

WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and M4A, from half a second up to ten minutes and 25 MB. Batches of up to 50 files are accepted.

Where does my audio go?

Uploads are stored on this server so they can be analysed, played back and reviewed. The Teachable Machine model runs in your own browser. No third-party cloud service classifies the audio.

What happens when the two models disagree?

The event is marked as a disagreement and goes to the review queue, where a reviewer listens to the segment and confirms or corrects the category.

Can the models be retrained?

Yes. Both are trained on the same dataset and splits: python -m python_models.train for the Python model and python -m scripts.train_gtm_model for the Teachable Machine network. The app picks up new model files without a restart.
